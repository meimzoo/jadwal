About Jadwal

Why Jadwal?

Founder & CEO Maryam Amir

I'm a Computer Science student and the founder of Jadwal. I started this project because I enjoy going out but hate the endless "Where should I go" loop that never ends. I wanted something quick, simple, and stress-free! That's how Jadwal was born.

Vision:

To make social planning effortless and stress-free by helping people find outings that match their mood, budget, and vibe with just one tap.

Mission:

To become the go-to lifestyle app for young people who want quick, easy, and reliable ways to plan group or solo outings anywhere, anytime.

Core Values:

  • Simplicity
  • Inclusivity
  • Smart Social Connections
  • Respecting time & money
